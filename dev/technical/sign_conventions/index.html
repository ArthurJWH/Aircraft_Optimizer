Sign Conventions & Reference Frames

This page documents the geometric axes, aerodynamic sign conventions, flow angles, and moment definitions used throughout Aircraft_Optimizer.

Aircraft Body Coordinate System

The aircraft geometry and panel coordinates are defined in a right-handed orthogonal Cartesian reference frame centered at the root leading edge of the primary lifting surface:

  • +X (Aft): Points longitudinally along the fuselage towards the tail.
  • +Y (Starboard): Points spanwise along the right wing.
  • +Z (Upward): Points vertically upward completing the right-handed system.

Surface Symmetry

For symmetric surfaces with mirror_xz = true, the defined geometry represents the starboard (right) semi-span with spanwise fraction $y \in [0, 1]$. The port (left) semi-span is automatically generated by reflecting Cartesian coordinates across the $Y = 0$ plane (global $Y \to -Y$).

Flow Angles & Wind Direction

The freestream velocity vector $\vec{V}_\infty$ is defined in body axes by two aerodynamic angles:

  • Angle of Attack ($\alpha$): Pitch angle between the fuselage centerline and the freestream velocity projected onto the XZ-plane (positive denotes nose-up pitch into the wind).
  • Sideslip Angle ($\beta$): Sideslip angle between the freestream velocity and the XZ symmetry plane (positive denotes freestream arriving from the starboard side).

In body Cartesian coordinates, the unit freestream velocity vector is:

\[\vec{u}_\infty = \begin{bmatrix} -\cos\alpha \cos\beta \\ -\sin\beta \\ -\sin\alpha \cos\beta \end{bmatrix}\]

Surface Geometric Distributions

All geometric variations are defined as continuous functions of the non-dimensional semi-span station $y \in [0, 1]$ (where $y = 0$ is root and $y = 1$ is tip):

1. Chord chord(y)

Local section chord length in meters ($c(y) \ge 0$).

2. Geometric Twist twist(y)

Section incidence angle in degrees.

  • Positive twist: Rotates the section leading edge upward (pitch-up / washin).
  • Negative twist: Rotates the section leading edge downward (pitch-down / washout).
  • Pivot: Rotation occurs about the chordwise fraction tw_center (default: 0.25 / quarter-chord).

3. Sweep sweep(y)

Local sweep angle $\Lambda(y)$ in degrees of the reference chord line sw_center (default: 0.25).

  • Positive sweep: Displaces the station aft (+X) along the span.
  • Offset calculation: Physical longitudinal displacement is obtained by integrating $\tan\Lambda(y)$:

    \[\Delta x(y) = \frac{b}{2} \int_0^y \tan\Lambda(\eta) \, d\eta\]

Sweep Limit

Local sweep angle must satisfy $|\Lambda(y)| < 90^\circ$ to prevent singular coordinate integration.

4. Dihedral dihedral(y)

Local dihedral angle $\Gamma(y)$ in degrees.

  • Positive dihedral: Deflects the station upward (+Z) along the span.
  • Offset calculation: Physical vertical displacement is obtained by integrating $\tan\Gamma(y)$:

    \[\Delta z(y) = \frac{b}{2} \int_0^y \tan\Gamma(\eta) \, d\eta\]

5. Vertical Surfaces (vertical = true)

For vertical tails or winglets:

  • The semi-span station $y \in [0, 1]$ extends vertically along the +Z axis (root at bottom, tip at top).
  • "Dihedral" deflections apply laterally along the +Y axis.

Normalization & Reference Quantities

Non-dimensional coefficients are normalized by dynamic pressure $q = \frac{1}{2} \rho V_\infty^2$ and reference geometry from the primary lifting surface (plane.surfaces[1]):

\[C_L = \frac{L}{q S_\text{ref}}, \quad C_D = \frac{D}{q S_\text{ref}}, \quad C_Y = \frac{F_Y}{q S_\text{ref}}\]

\[C_M = \frac{M}{q S_\text{ref} c_\text{ref}}, \quad C_{Ml} = \frac{M_l}{q S_\text{ref} b_\text{ref}}, \quad C_N = \frac{N}{q S_\text{ref} b_\text{ref}}\]
